# V5.2 — Power and Analogue Preflight Register

## Status

Open calculation register.

## Purpose

Prevent schematic capture from hiding unresolved current, thermal, gain, polarity or startup assumptions.

## Rail budget

The following must be calculated from datasheet maximums, not typical values alone.

| Rail | Loads | Calculated maximum | Design margin | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| +12 V analogue | OPA1679 stages, SSI2164, analogue signal path | Pending | Minimum 25% | Open |
| −12 V analogue | OPA1679 stages, SSI2164, analogue signal path | Pending | Minimum 25% | Open |
| 5.5 V preregulator | TPS7A2050 input plus losses | Pending | Minimum 25% | Open |
| quiet 5 V | PCM3168A analogue supply | Pending | Minimum 25% | Open |
| 3.3 V | MCU, codec digital/interface, DAC, supervisor, watchdog, LEDs/control | Pending | Minimum 30% transient margin | Open |

## Mandatory calculations

### 3.3 V path

- STM32H743 worst-case operating current at the intended clock/voltage scale.
- PCM3168A digital/interface current.
- MCP4728 current.
- supervisor and watchdog current.
- state-light and control-current allowance.
- regulator efficiency at minimum and maximum expected load.
- startup/transient margin.
- regulator junction-temperature estimate.

### Quiet 5 V path

- PCM3168A maximum analogue current.
- TPS7A2050 dropout at required load.
- preregulator tolerance and ripple.
- LDO dissipation at maximum input and load.
- codec analogue-decoupling and local bulk requirement.

### ±12 V analogue path

- complete OPA1679 amplifier count.
- quiescent current for every package.
- SSI2164 current.
- output-load allowance.
- LED or control loads that must not share quiet audio rails without justification.
- total positive/negative rail asymmetry.

## SSI2164 safety calculation

Before schematic acceptance, record:

- control-current polarity;
- control law and attenuation direction;
- voltage/current range needed for maximum attenuation;
- MCP4728-to-SSI2164 scaling;
- hardware-clamp circuit that forces maximum attenuation with MCU absent;
- clamp behaviour during partial power and rail collapse;
- wet/Return gain at clamp assertion;
- recovery ramp after clamp release.

The schematic must not rely on firmware or stored DAC values for silence.

## Return limiter calculation

The Return loop remains non-compliant until the schematic proves:

```text
raw Return DAC
→ Break
→ nonlinear Return stage
→ hard limiter
→ RETURN_LIMITED
```

Required evidence:

- maximum small-signal loop gain;
- limiting threshold;
- limiting behaviour with MCU frozen;
- limiting behaviour with codec full-scale output;
- output/ingress headroom at worst control settings;
- no alternate net path around the limiter;
- reset and fault forcing Return attenuation.

## Watchdog and supervisor

Record:

- TPS3431 timeout selection and tolerance;
- heartbeat source independent of successful audio output;
- active-low open-drain fault pull-up domain;
- fault-combination logic;
- TPS3808 reset threshold/delay;
- behaviour during slow rail rise and brownout;
- guaranteed assertion of codec mute/reset and VCA clamp.

## Package/buildability checks

- TPS3431 VSON thermal-pad land pattern verified.
- stencil/reflow requirement recorded.
- probe points provided for watchdog input/output.
- no reflow-only component hidden beneath panel hardware.
- 0805 default maintained except documented dense-area exceptions.

## Closure rule

This register closes only when every calculation has a source, equation, assumptions, result and margin.

PCB work and purchasing remain blocked while any safety or thermal row is open.
