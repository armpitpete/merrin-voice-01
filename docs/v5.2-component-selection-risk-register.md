# V5.2 — Component-Selection Risk Register

## Status

Architecture review register.

The selected component architecture is acceptable for schematic capture only if the following items remain visible and are closed before schematic acceptance.

None of these items authorises PCB placement or routing.

## R1 — PCM3168A clock-mode validation

**Risk**

The preferred clock plan is:

```text
48 kHz LRCLK
12.288 MHz BCLK
24.576 MHz MCLK
8 × 32-bit TDM slots
```

The exact PCM3168A serial-format and system-clock mode must support that combination.

**Required closure**

- identify the exact PCM3168A mode/register settings
- verify MCU SAI can generate the complete clock relationship
- record the selected data-line direction and slot allocation
- confirm codec reset/mute sequencing around clock startup

**Owner lane**

V5.2 schematic capture.

## R2 — STM32H743 SRAM and DMA placement

**Risk**

The two-second 32-bit mono history uses 384,000 bytes of AXI SRAM.

Incorrect linker or DMA placement could leave insufficient margin or place DMA buffers in inaccessible memory.

**Required closure**

- produce a memory-region table
- reserve the circular history explicitly
- place DMA buffers in DMA-accessible SRAM
- prove stack, heap, control state, and audio work buffers fit outside the protected history region
- retain at least 64 KB uncommitted safety margin across usable SRAM after the first complete firmware link

**Owner lane**

Firmware architecture after schematic pin/clock acceptance.

## R3 — SSI2164 attenuation polarity and range

**Risk**

An incorrect control-voltage polarity or scaling could make reset increase gain rather than force attenuation.

**Required closure**

- calculate the datasheet transfer function
- state the control voltage for unity and maximum safe attenuation
- define the MCP4728 scaling network
- define a hardware clamp that wins against MCU/DAC output
- verify safe state with MCU absent, DAC absent, and watchdog asserted

**Owner lane**

V5.2 schematic capture and independent Return-safety review.

## R4 — MCP4728 startup/EEPROM state

**Risk**

Stored or default DAC outputs could request audible wet/Return gain during power-up.

**Required closure**

- hardware attenuation clamp remains active until SAFE-01 release
- stored EEPROM settings are either unused or explicitly programmed to the safe state
- power sequencing is tested with MCU held reset
- I2C failure leaves the instrument attenuated

**Owner lane**

V5.2 schematic and later bring-up firmware.

## R5 — TPS3431 VSON assembly

**Risk**

VSON-8 is less accessible for hand soldering and visual inspection than the preferred leaded packages.

**Required closure**

- verified land pattern and exposed-pad requirements
- stencil/reflow or assembly-service process stated
- watchdog input/output test points accessible
- device not placed beneath panel hardware
- replacement considered only if an active leaded-package watchdog meets the same safety need without weakening independence

**Owner lane**

Schematic footprint review and later PCB planning.

## R6 — Codec 5 V rail dropout and heat

**Risk**

The planned 5.5 V pre-rail gives limited LDO headroom. Incorrect load or thermal assumptions could cause dropout or excess dissipation.

**Required closure**

- calculate PCM3168A analogue current including margin
- calculate TPS7A2050 dropout at worst load and temperature
- calculate LDO dissipation from the chosen pre-rail
- calculate TPS62160 inductor/current/ripple requirements
- verify startup and transient margin

**Owner lane**

V5.2 power-sheet design.

## R7 — 3.3 V rail current budget

**Risk**

MCU, codec digital, MCP4728, LEDs, supervisor, watchdog, and control circuitry may create higher peak current than a rough estimate suggests.

**Required closure**

- complete worst-case and typical current table
- include MCU transient load and LED limits
- include regulator efficiency and thermal margin
- reserve at least 25% regulator current margin

**Owner lane**

V5.2 power-sheet design.

## R8 — WQP518MA switching and footprint

**Risk**

A footprint or switched-contact assumption based on an older PJ301BM drawing could silently invert the no-cable state or misalign the panel.

**Required closure**

- verify WQP518MA manufacturer drawing
- verify tip, sleeve, and switched-contact pin numbers
- verify footprint dimensions independently
- verify panel axis, nut/washer, insertion depth, and adjacent-control clearance
- document the intended no-cable AUDIO IN state

**Owner lane**

V5.2 schematic symbol review and later mechanical/PCB review.

## R9 — OPA1679 channel and current count

**Risk**

The architecture uses OPA1679 across many analogue functions. An incomplete channel count could cause late package growth or excessive ±12 V current.

**Required closure**

- count every amplifier channel by sheet
- add at least one spare channel only where it does not create an unstable unused amplifier
- calculate typical and worst-case rail current
- terminate every unused amplifier in a stable datasheet-compliant state

**Owner lane**

V5.2 schematic capture.

## R10 — STM32H743VIT6 pin allocation

**Risk**

LQFP-100 may have sufficient total pins but the chosen SAI, clocks, ADC channels, debug, buttons, LEDs, resets, and buses may conflict on alternate functions.

**Required closure**

- produce a complete pin-allocation table before schematic completion
- verify all SAI/TDM signals share a valid peripheral configuration
- verify eight control ADC inputs are available
- preserve SWD and reset access
- document all unused pins and boot straps

**Owner lane**

V5.2 MCU-sheet design.

## R11 — Unused PCM3168A channels

**Risk**

Unused converter inputs may float or unused outputs may produce unwanted noise/current if not configured correctly.

**Required closure**

- identify every unused ADC and DAC channel
- apply the datasheet-defined unused-input treatment
- mute or power down unused channels where supported
- confirm no unused output enters an analogue mixer

**Owner lane**

V5.2 codec-sheet design.

## R12 — Return limiter independence

**Risk**

A limiter implemented only in firmware or only through normal VCA control would not satisfy the accepted hardware-safety architecture.

**Required closure**

- RET-02 is an analogue hardware limiter
- `RETURN_LIMITED` is the only feedback-audio net exported from the Return sheet
- limiter remains active with MCU, codec, or control DAC faulted
- complete loop-gain calculation and worst-setting bench test are required

**Owner lane**

V5.2 schematic and independent safety review.

## Architecture acceptance rule

PR acceptance means:

```text
The selected parts and sheet structure are suitable to begin schematic capture.
```

It does not mean:

```text
all schematic calculations are complete
all footprints are approved
PCB work may begin
parts may be purchased
```
