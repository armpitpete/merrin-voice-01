# V5.2 — Prototype A Power, Current and Thermal Budget

## Status

Draft for acceptance before native schematic capture.

## Purpose

Turn the accepted power architecture into explicit rail loads, regulator targets, divider values, inductor requirements and thermal checks.

This document does not authorise PCB placement, routing, fabrication or purchasing.

```text
protected +12 V
  ├── TPS62160 → 3.3 V digital

                      ↓
```

# 3.3 V digital rail

## Load budget

| Load | Design current |
|---|---:|
| STM32H743VIT6 at 300 MHz VOS2 | 200 mA |
| PCM3168A digital supply | 130 mA |
| MCP4728 | 1.4 mA |
| TMUX1574 safe-control selector | 0.1 mA allowance |
| TPS3808 + TPS3431 | 0.1 mA allowance |
| SLS-1 LEDs, total limited current | 20 mA |
| I2C pull-ups, GPIO loading and small control logic reserve | 20 mA |
| **Calculated design subtotal** | **371.6 mA** |

Locked regulator design load:

```text
500 mA
```

Margin above the subtotal:

```text
500 mA − 371.6 mA = 128.4 mA
128.4 / 371.6 = 34.5% reserve
```

The TPS62160 is rated for up to 1 A output. A 500 mA Prototype A design load therefore uses no more than half of the regulator's nominal current capability.

## MCU operating assumption

Prototype A firmware targets:

```text
300 MHz
VOS2
```

The 200 mA allocation is deliberately above the approximately 154 mA high-temperature maximum published for the selected 300 MHz all-peripherals condition at 85 °C.

This is a power-budget assumption, not permission to run every peripheral continuously.

If later firmware requires a higher voltage scale or materially greater clock/current, R7 must reopen.

## Output divider

TPS62160 adjustable output relation:

```text
VOUT = 0.8 V × (1 + RTOP / RBOTTOM)
```

Selected 1% values:

```text
RTOP    = 374 kΩ
RBOTTOM = 120 kΩ
```

Nominal result:

```text
VOUT = 0.8 × (1 + 374 / 120)
     = 3.293 V
```

Worst resistor-tolerance bounds, ignoring the regulator reference tolerance:

```text
approximately 3.244 V to 3.344 V
```

The native schematic must add the regulator reference tolerance before final rail acceptance.

## Inductor and ripple target

Selected starting values:

```text
L    = 2.2 µH
COUT = 22 µF effective minimum target
CIN  = 10 µF local minimum target
fSW  ≈ 2.25 MHz
VIN  = 12 V nominal
```

At 3.293 V and 500 mA:

```text
D ≈ 3.293 / 12 = 0.274
ΔIL ≈ 0.483 A
IPEAK ≈ 0.500 + 0.483 / 2
      ≈ 0.741 A
```

Inductor requirement:

```text
2.2 µH
saturation current ≥ 1.3 A
preferred practical target around 1.8–2.0 A
low DCR
```

The final inductor and capacitor derating must be checked in the native power-sheet calculation.

# Quiet 5 V PCM3168A analogue rail

## Codec load

PCM3168A analogue current:

```text
typical: 162 mA
maximum: 210 mA
```

Locked quiet-rail design load with 25% margin:

```text
210 mA × 1.25 = 262.5 mA
rounded design load = 265 mA
```

TPS7A2050 is rated for 300 mA, leaving 35 mA between the design load and regulator rating.

This rail is dedicated to the PCM3168A analogue supply and its local support network. Other loads may not be added without reopening R6.

## Pre-regulator divider

Selected 1% TPS62160 values:

```text
RTOP    = 698 kΩ
RBOTTOM = 120 kΩ
```

Nominal result:

```text
VOUT = 0.8 × (1 + 698 / 120)
     = 5.453 V
```

Resistor-tolerance bounds, excluding regulator reference tolerance:

```text
approximately 5.361 V to 5.547 V
```

The schematic must prove that the combined regulator-reference and resistor tolerances do not exceed the TPS7A20 input range or remove required dropout margin.

## TPS7A2050 dropout and dissipation

Published maximum dropout at 300 mA for the selected package is approximately:

```text
145 mV
```

Nominal LDO headroom:

```text
5.453 V − 5.000 V = 0.453 V
```

This exceeds the published maximum-dropout figure at the larger 300 mA test load.

Nominal dissipation at maximum PCM3168A load:

```text
0.453 V × 0.210 A = 0.095 W
```

At the 265 mA design load:

```text
0.453 V × 0.265 A = 0.120 W
```

Using the published DBV-package junction-to-ambient figure of approximately 187.1 °C/W as a conservative first estimate:

```text
ΔTJ ≈ 0.120 W × 187.1 °C/W
    ≈ 22.5 °C
```

A conservative 0.5 V drop would produce approximately 0.133 W and a 24.8 °C estimated rise.

Final thermal acceptance requires the actual copper implementation and ambient target; this calculation only proves that the architecture is not intrinsically thermally unreasonable.

## Pre-regulator inductor target

At 5.453 V and 265 mA, with 12 V input, 2.2 µH and 2.25 MHz:

```text
D ≈ 5.453 / 12 = 0.454
ΔIL ≈ 0.601 A
IPEAK ≈ 0.265 + 0.601 / 2
      ≈ 0.566 A
```

Use the same minimum inductor rule:

```text
2.2 µH
saturation current ≥ 1.3 A
preferred practical target around 1.8–2.0 A
low DCR
```

## Quiet-rail filtering rules

- TPS62160 switching node must not share sensitive codec analogue-current paths.
- TPS7A2050 input and output capacitors must satisfy its stability requirements.
- PCM3168A analogue supply pins require local decoupling at each relevant supply group.
- The quiet 5 V net may not power LEDs, logic or panel controls.
- Test points are required before and after the LDO.

# ±12 V analogue rails

## OPA1679 allocation

The accepted channel plan uses:

```text
7 × OPA1679
28 amplifier channels
```

OPA1679 supply current:

```text
2.0 mA typical per channel
2.8 mA worst-case per channel across temperature
```

For 28 channels:

```text
typical: 28 × 2.0 mA = 56.0 mA per rail
worst:   28 × 2.8 mA = 78.4 mA per rail
```

## SSI2164 allocation

One SSI2164 in Class AB mode:

```text
typical approximately ±6 mA
maximum approximately ±8 mA
```

## Analogue-rail design allocation

Core analogue load before small references/protection:

```text
78.4 mA OPA1679 worst case
+ 8.0 mA SSI2164 maximum
= 86.4 mA per rail
```

Locked analogue-circuit design allowance:

```text
100 mA on +12 V analogue core
100 mA on −12 V analogue core
```

This includes approximately 13.6 mA per rail for references, clamp networks and small analogue support devices.

## Total bench-supply planning estimate

The +12 V rail also feeds both buck regulators.

Approximate +12 V input current at 85% conversion efficiency:

```text
3.3 V rail:
(3.3 V × 0.500 A) / (0.85 × 12 V)
≈ 162 mA

5.453 V pre-rail:
(5.453 V × 0.265 A) / (0.85 × 12 V)
≈ 142 mA
```

Buck-derived subtotal:

```text
approximately 304 mA from +12 V
```

Prototype bench-supply planning values:

```text
+12 V: 425 mA design allocation
−12 V: 110 mA design allocation
```

These are current-limited bring-up targets, not a final Eurorack power claim.

# Power-up and fault requirements

1. 3.3 V supervision must keep the MCU, codec control and safe-control release invalid until the rail is stable.
2. Quiet 5 V validity must be confirmed before codec reset release.
3. The independent analogue Present path may not depend on the digital rails becoming valid.
4. Loss of 3.3 V must force wet/Return attenuation through the external hardware safety structure.
5. The final output safety path must suppress any rail-collapse transient not covered by the normal wet VCA controls.
6. Every rail requires a labelled test point and current-limited first-power procedure.
