
# 3.3 V digital rail

## Load budget

| Load | Design current |
|---|---:|
| STM32H743VIT6 at 300 MHz VOS2 | 200 mA |
| PCM3168A digital supply | 130 mA |
| MCP4728 | 1.4 mA |
| TMUX1574 selector | 0.1 mA allowance |
| TPS3808 + TPS3431 | 0.1 mA allowance |
| SLS-1 LEDs, current limited total | 20 mA |
| I2C, GPIO and small-logic reserve | 20 mA |
| **Subtotal** | **371.6 mA** |

Locked design load:

```text
500 mA
```

Reserve:

```text
500 mA − 371.6 mA = 128.4 mA
128.4 / 371.6 = 34.5%
```

TPS62160 is rated for up to 1 A, so the 500 mA design load uses half of its nominal output-current capability.

## MCU assumption

Prototype A targets:

```text
300 MHz
VOS2
prototype ambient target no higher than 85 °C without reopening the budget
```

The 200 mA allowance exceeds the published approximately 154 mA maximum for the selected 300 MHz all-peripherals condition at 85 °C.

A higher voltage scale, clock or thermal target reopens R7.

## Output divider

TPS62160 relation:

```text
VOUT = 0.8 V × (1 + RTOP / RBOTTOM)
```

Selected 1% values:

```text
RTOP    = 374 kΩ
RBOTTOM = 120 kΩ
VOUT nominal = 3.293 V
```

Resistor-only tolerance range:

```text
approximately 3.244 V to 3.344 V
```

The native schematic must add regulator-reference tolerance.

## LC calculation

Locked starting values:

```text
L    = 2.2 µH
CIN  = 10 µF local minimum target
COUT = 22 µF effective target
fSW  ≈ 2.25 MHz
VIN  = 12 V nominal
```

At 3.293 V and 500 mA:

```text
D ≈ 0.274
ΔIL ≈ 0.483 A
IPEAK ≈ 0.741 A
IVALLEY ≈ 0.259 A
```

The positive valley is consistent with continuous-conduction PWM at the design load.

Inductor requirement:

```text
2.2 µH
saturation current ≥ 1.3 A
preferred practical rating around 1.8–2.0 A
low DCR
```

# Quiet 5 V PCM3168A analogue rail

## Codec load

```text
PCM3168A analogue current:
typical = 162 mA
maximum = 210 mA
```

With 25% design margin:

```text
210 mA × 1.25 = 262.5 mA
locked design load = 265 mA
```

TPS7A2050 is rated for 300 mA. The quiet rail is dedicated to the PCM3168A analogue supply and local support network.

## Pre-regulator divider

Selected 1% values:

```text
RTOP    = 698 kΩ
RBOTTOM = 120 kΩ
VOUT nominal = 5.453 V
```

Resistor-only tolerance range:

```text
approximately 5.361 V to 5.547 V
```

The native schematic must include regulator-reference tolerance and verify TPS7A20 input/dropout limits.

## TPS7A2050 dropout and heat

Published maximum dropout at 300 mA for the selected package is approximately:

```text
145 mV
```

Nominal headroom:

```text
5.453 V − 5.000 V = 0.453 V
```

Dissipation:

```text
at 210 mA: 0.453 V × 0.210 A = 0.095 W
at 265 mA: 0.453 V × 0.265 A = 0.120 W
```

Using approximately 187.1 °C/W as a conservative first package estimate:

```text
ΔTJ ≈ 0.120 W × 187.1 °C/W
    ≈ 22.5 °C
```

Final thermal acceptance depends on actual copper and ambient conditions.

## Corrected pre-regulator LC calculation

The provisional 2.2 µH value gives approximately 0.601 A ripple at 265 mA, making the calculated valley slightly negative. TPS62160 can enter power-save/discontinuous operation seamlessly, but that is not the preferred stated operating point for the quiet codec pre-rail.

The corrected starting value is:

```text
L    = 3.3 µH
CIN  = 10 µF local minimum target
COUT = 22 µF effective target
fSW  ≈ 2.25 MHz
```

At 5.453 V and 265 mA:

```text
D ≈ 0.454
ΔIL ≈ 0.401 A
IPEAK ≈ 0.465 A
IVALLEY ≈ 0.065 A
```

This keeps the first-pass design in continuous conduction at the locked load.

The TPS62160 datasheet lists 3.3 µH inductors and proven 3.3 µH/output-capacitor combinations.

Inductor requirement:

```text
3.3 µH
saturation current ≥ 1.3 A
preferred selected part ≥ 1.5 A
low DCR
```

## Quiet-rail rules

- switching-current loops must stay away from codec analogue/reference nodes;
- TPS7A2050 capacitors must satisfy its stability requirements;
- every PCM3168A analogue supply group requires local decoupling;
- quiet 5 V may not power LEDs or general logic;
- test points are required before and after the LDO.

```text

+ 8.0 mA SSI2164 maximum
+ 1.0 mA OPA4196 design maximum

```

The remaining approximately 12.6 mA per rail covers references, clamps and small analogue support devices. Exceeding it reopens the budget.

```text
(3.3 V × 0.500 A) / (0.85 × 12 V)

(5.453 V × 0.265 A) / (0.85 × 12 V)

```

```text
approximately 304 mA from +12 V
```

Locked current-limited bench planning:

```text
+12 V = 425 mA
−12 V = 110 mA
```

These are prototype bring-up allocations, not final Eurorack power claims.

# Fault requirements

1. Digital reset and safe-control release remain invalid until 3.3 V is stable.
2. Quiet 5 V validity is required before codec reset release.
3. Direct analogue Present does not depend on digital rails.
4. Digital-rail loss asserts codec and wet/output suppression.
5. Final output safety must catch rail-collapse transients not handled by normal wet control.
6. Every rail requires labelled test access and current-limited first power-up.
