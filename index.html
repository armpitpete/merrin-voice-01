Constrained Grief

A clickable guide to the five-module Merrin Voice 01 system. It explains the signal path, the practical job of each module, the Merrin Link bus, and how front-panel patching can override the hidden rear normals.

Merrin Link Bus

The rear bus lets the five modules behave as one pre-patched mono voice when the jumpers are enabled.

Linked mode: pitch, gate, and audio normals are active through the rear Merrin Link bus.

Front patch rule: if a front-panel cable is inserted, it can override the hidden Merrin Link connection.

Current boundary

This app explains the instrument and includes one tiny audio preview. It does not save patches or simulate full electronics.

Front-panel override examples

Merrin Link gives the default path. A front-panel cable can deliberately replace part of that path.

Example 1 — External pitch overrides Merrin pitch

  1. Default: MV-02 → PITCH → MV-01
  2. Front patch: plug external pitch into MV-01 1V/OCT.
  3. Result: MV-01 follows the external pitch instead of the Merrin Link pitch normal.

Example 2 — External audio enters the space stage

  1. Default: MV-03 → AUD_B → MF-01
  2. Front patch: plug external audio into MF-01 AUDIO IN.
  3. Result: MF-01 processes the external audio instead of the normal Merrin Voice path.
