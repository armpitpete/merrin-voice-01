Constrained Grief

A clickable guide to the five-module Merrin Voice 01 system. It explains the signal path, the practical job of each module, and the emotional role each stage adds to the voice.

Merrin Link Bus

The rear bus lets the five modules behave as one pre-patched mono voice when the jumpers are enabled.

PITCHQuantizer pitch to oscillator
GATEGate signal to Lingering Voice
AUD_AOscillator to VCA
AUD_BVCA to reverb
AUD_CReverb to delay
GNDShared reference ground

Jumpers on: linked voice mode. Jumpers off: each module works independently through its front-panel jacks.
